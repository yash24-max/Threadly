# Vision — Threadly

## Product name
**Threadly** — *"Build AI chatbots that remember every thread."*

The "thread" metaphor scales: single-channel chat → omnichannel conversation threads → unified customer CRM threads. One brand for three phases of growth.

## Problem
Businesses miss leads and give bad support because:
- No AI-powered first response on their website
- Manual, slow reply flows
- No memory of what the customer said before
- Every tool is separate — chatbot here, inbox there, CRM elsewhere

## Solution
Threadly starts as a **focused AI chatbot builder + website widget**. Businesses build a flow in a visual canvas, upload their knowledge base, and embed the widget in minutes. The AI handles conversations with memory and RAG-backed context.

## Positioning
Not "another chatbot" — **the AI assistant that actually knows your business**, answers from your docs, and hands off to a human when needed.

## Target customer (Phase 0)
Small-medium businesses with a website who want:
- Instant AI responses to common questions
- Lead capture
- 24/7 availability without hiring a support agent

Vertical sweet spots: clinics, real-estate agencies, coaching businesses, service SMBs.

## Long-term vision
Phase 0 → Chatbot Builder + Website Widget  
Phase 1 → Omnichannel (WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram)  
Phase 2 → Shared inbox + CRM Lite  
Phase 3 → AI Copilots + Workflow Automation  
Phase 4 → Full AI Employee Platform  

## Differentiators
1. **AI memory across sessions** — visitor context persists and is used in every reply
2. **RAG from your own docs** — answer from your PDFs/URLs, with citations
3. **Hybrid AI + Human** — smooth handoff, agent can take over any conversation
4. **Developer-friendly embed** — single `<script>` snippet, fully customizable
5. **Built for SMBs** — 5-minute onboarding, no technical skill needed to build a bot
